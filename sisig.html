Kusina Filipina

Sisig

Sisig is a sizzling Filipino dish made from finely chopped pork, usually from the head and liver, seasoned with calamansi, onions, and chili peppers. Crispy, tangy, and spicy, it is a favorite pulutan.

Nutritional Information

Rich in protein from pork, moderate fat content, and a good source of flavor through spices and condiments. Calories depend on oil and meat used.

Sisig Recipe

This recipe produces crispy, flavorful sisig perfect for serving hot.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb pork cheeks, finely chopped
  • 1/2 lb pork liver, optional
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2-3 green chili peppers, chopped
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1-2 tbsp calamansi juice
  • 1 tsp ground black pepper
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • Optional: mayonnaise for topping

Instructions

  1. Boil pork cheeks and liver until tender. Drain and chop finely.
  2. Heat oil in a pan. Sauté onions and chili peppers.
  3. Add chopped pork and liver. Stir-fry until crispy.
  4. Add soy sauce, calamansi juice, and black pepper. Mix well.
  5. Serve hot on a sizzling plate. Optional: add mayonnaise on top.
