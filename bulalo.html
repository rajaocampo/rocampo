Kusina Filipina

Bulalo

Bulalo is a classic Filipino soup dish renowned for its rich,
clear broth and tender beef shanks, often including bone marrow. Originating from the Southern Luzon region, particularly Batangas,
this hearty and comforting stew is simmered for hours until the beef is fall-off-the-bone tender
and the marrow is buttery soft. It's typically served hot,
garnished with fresh vegetables, and is a popular dish, especially during cooler weather or as a celebratory meal

Bulalo Recipe

This recipe yields a tender, melts in your
mouth beef shank and a deeply flavored
broth

Ingredients

  • Beef: 3 pounds bone-in beef shank (cross-section cuts), with marrow bones preferred
  • Water: 8 cups, plus up to 4 more cups for simmering
  • Aromatics:
  • 2 thumb-sized pieces of ginger, sliced
  • 1 large onion, sliced or quartered
  • Seasoning:
  • 2 teaspoons beef base or bouillon
  • Fish sauce (patis), salt, and freshly cracked black pepper, to taste
  • Neutral oil, for searing and sautéing
  • 2 large potatoes, quartered
  • 2 ears of corn, cut into 2-inch pieces
  • A bunch of green beans, trimmed
  • ½ head of cabbage, cut into large pieces
  • A bunch of bok choy (or pechay), cut in half if large
  • Garnish (Optional): Chopped cilantro stems

Instructions

    1. Blanch Beef (Optional: Boil beef shanks in water to remove impurities). Drain and rinse.
    2. Sear Meat (Optional): Season beef with salt and pepper; Sear in oil until browned, then set aside.
    3. Sauté Aromatics: Sauté ginger, onion, and garlic until softened.
    4. Add Beef and Water: Return beef to pot, add 8 cups of water, and bring to a near boil.
    5. Skim Scum: Remove scum from the surface as it appears.
    6. Season Broth: Add beef bouillon, salt, and pepper to the pot.
    7. Simmer Beef: Cover and simmer for 2-2.5 hours, until beef is very tender.
      Add water as needed, adjust seasoning with fish sauce, salt, and pepper to taste.
    8. Add Potatoes and Corn: Add potatoes and corn; simmer for 10 minutes, until tender.
    9. Add Green Beans: Add green beans; simmer for 3 minutes, until tender.
    10. Add Cabbage and Bok Choy: Add cabbage and bok choy, simmer for 2 minutes. Turn off heat.
    11. Garnish and Serve: Garnish with cilantro stems (optional). Serve hot with rice and fish sauce or calamansi.
