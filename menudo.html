Kusina Filipina

Menudo

Menudo is a classic Filipino pork stew with liver, potatoes, carrots, bell peppers, and raisins in a savory tomato sauce. It's hearty, flavorful, and commonly served during family gatherings or special occasions.

Nutritional Information

Good source of protein from pork and liver. Contains vitamins and fiber from vegetables. Sodium and fat depend on seasonings and cuts of meat used.

Menudo Recipe

This recipe yields a rich, savory, and colorful Filipino stew perfect with steamed rice.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb pork, diced
  • ½ lb pork liver, diced
  • 2 medium potatoes, diced
  • 2 medium carrots, diced
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • ½ cup raisins
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • Oil, for sautéing

Instructions

  1. Sauté garlic and onion in oil until fragrant.
  2. Add diced pork; cook until lightly browned.
  3. Add liver and cook 2–3 minutes.
  4. Pour in tomato sauce and soy sauce; simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Add potatoes and carrots; cook until tender.
  6. Add bell peppers and raisins; simmer another 5 minutes.
  7. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Serve hot with rice.
