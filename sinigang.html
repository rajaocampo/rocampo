Kusina Filipina

Sinigang

Sinigang is a popular Filipino sour soup, usually made with pork, shrimp, or fish, flavored with tamarind. It features vegetables like water spinach, radish, eggplant, and okra, resulting in a tangy and comforting dish.

Nutritional Information

High in vitamins from vegetables and moderate protein from meat or seafood. Sodium content depends on fish sauce or seasoning used.

Sinigang Recipe

This recipe creates a tangy, savory, and comforting Filipino soup.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb pork ribs or pork belly
  • 8 cups water
  • 1 medium onion, quartered
  • 2 medium tomatoes, quartered
  • 1 thumb-sized ginger, sliced
  • 1 pack tamarind soup base or fresh tamarind
  • 1 cup radish, sliced
  • 1 cup water spinach (kangkong)
  • 1 cup eggplant, sliced
  • 1 cup okra, trimmed
  • Fish sauce (patis), salt, and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Boil pork with onion, tomatoes, and ginger until tender.
  2. Add tamarind base or fresh tamarind; simmer for 5–10 minutes.
  3. Add vegetables gradually: radish first, then eggplant, okra, and kangkong.
  4. Season with fish sauce, salt, and pepper.
  5. Simmer until vegetables are cooked but still crisp.
  6. Serve hot with steamed rice.
