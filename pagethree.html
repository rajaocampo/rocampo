Bulalo

Bulalo is a classic Filipino soup dish renowned for its rich, clear broth and tender beef shanks with bone marrow.

Nutritional Information

Exact nutritional values vary depending on ingredients and portions.

Bulalo Recipe

This recipe yields a tender beef shank and rich broth.

Ingredients

  • 3 lbs beef shank
  • 8 cups water
  • Garlic
  • Onion
  • Potatoes
  • Corn
  • Cabbage

Instructions

  1. Blanch beef.
  2. Sauté aromatics.
  3. Simmer for 2 hours.
  4. Add vegetables.
  5. Season and serve.
